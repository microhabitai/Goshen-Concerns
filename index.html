Our Story

About Goshen Concerns

Goshen Concerns Computers Limited has been a trusted business centre in Jos, Plateau State for many years. Located at Old Airport Junction, we have served thousands of individuals, students, businesses and organizations with professional printing, documentation and digital services.

Beyond business centre services, we are passionate about digital literacy and have trained hundreds of students in computer skills that have opened doors for careers and self-employment.
